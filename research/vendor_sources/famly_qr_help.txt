QR Code Check-in/out Screen
Written by
Josie
March 2, 2026
Table of contents
Checking children in and out of the nursery is made simple with the separate Check-in/out screen. The screen can be set up to allow parents to check in their child with additional security features such as a PIN code and now a QR code. This article outlines how to set up and use the Check-in/out screen with the
QR Code Check-in/out feature
.
How does the QR Code Check-in/out feature work?
Once the QR Code Check-in/out feature has been toggled on for your center via your
Settings
, we recommend placing a tablet that's logged into the Check-in/out screen at the entrance in a spot that easy for parents to access.
The tablet will show a QR code for parents to scan with their own devices. This QR code will automatically update every 5 seconds to ensure that parents cannot share a picture of it with others (such as with a PIN code that can easily be shared or forgotten) or check their children in or out unless they're inside the center.
Scanning the QR code will lead parents to complete the Check in/out flow from their own devices. Parents have 15-30 minutes to complete the Check in/out flow before the process times out and they need to scan the QR code again.
Once complete, their child will be checked in and this will be reflected in the room overview and the activity feed for that child.
How to set up QR Code Check-in/out
The QR Code Check-in/out feature will automatically appear once you've toggled it on in
Settings →
Check in/out
.
Once you've toggled on the QR check in feature, log into your Check-in/out screen by clicking on
Show password
and entering it after clicking
Go to Famly Check-in
. If the QR code doesn't show right away, log out and in again to refresh your Check-in/out screen.
Please note, if you turn off your tablet in the afternoon or evening or log out of the Check-in/out screen, you’ll need to log back in the next day. You can do this by going to the icon you added on your home screen and using the password found in
Settings →
Check in/out
(this password will update every day).
Getting parents started
Let parents know that they'll need a smartphone with access to a camera in order to use the QR Code Check-in/out screen. It is important that they
update their app
to the latest version of Famly This can be done in the app store on their smartphone.
If parents choose to use the camera app on their smartphone, they don't need to do any further setup and can get scanning right away.
If they use the in-app scanner within the Famly app, they'll need to give Famly permission to use the camera by toggling this on in their phone settings as they would do with any other app.
Printing the QR code
If you'd prefer to have a static QR code for parents to scan, you can choose to print it out. This is a great option if your center does not have a digital tablet at the entrance.
To print your QR code, head to
Settings →
Check in/out
and scroll down to the
Print QR code
section. Click on
Print
from here to open your print options. If you'd like to print a new QR code, simply click on
Refresh QR code
and click on
Print
again. A new QR code will be generated.
If you'd like to use the digital QR code on a tablet as well as a printed version elsewhere at your center, you can, of course, use both methods at the same time.
QR Code Check-in and PIN Check-in
If you'd like to use both the PIN Check-in/out screen and the QR code Check-in/out screen, you can enable both of these option by toggling on
PIN code required for check in
and
QR check in
in
Settings →
Check in/out.

How to disable QR Code Check-in/out
You can switch off the QR Code Check-in/out feature by going to
Settings →
Check in/out
and toggling off the QR Code Check in there. If you are unable to toggle this feature off, or if you have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to
support@famly.co
.
FAQs
If a parent or member of staff run into any issues using the QR Code Check-in/out screen, try asking the following questions to troubleshoot:
Have they updated their app to the latest version?

support@famly.co
