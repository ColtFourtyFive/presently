Attendance Overview
Attendance Overview
How attendance works in Playground for students and staff, and where to find it
Written by
Zach Fortin
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Table of contents
A quick tour of how attendance works in Playground — what gets tracked, where it lives, and how it connects to the rest of your account.
General Information
Playground tracks
two kinds of attendance
— student
check-in
/
check-out
and staff
clock-in
/
clock-out
. They're recorded separately and reported on separately.
Attendance can be recorded from the
admin dashboard
, the
Playground
mobile app, or a shared
Kiosk
device. All three write to the same record, so it doesn't matter which one your school uses day to day.
Every check-in and check-out is
timestamped and attributed
to whoever signed — a family member or a staff member. That signature trail is what licensing reports are built from.
Attendance is
per-site
. Each school has its own roster, its own Kiosk, and its own attendance settings.
Attendance doesn't just sit in reports — it feeds
classroom ratios
,
overage billing
,
subsidy submissions
, and
payroll
. More on that below.
Admins can edit or add attendance records after the fact, so a missed check-out doesn't leave a permanent gap in your records.
How Attendance Is Recorded
There are four ways attendance gets into Playground, and most schools use a mix of them.
Kiosk Mode
— a locked-down tablet at your front door. Families sign in and out by scanning a
QR code
or entering their
PIN
. This is the most common setup for schools that want families signing for their own children.
Teacher check-in on mobile
— teachers check students in and out from the
Roster
in the mobile app, one at a time or in bulk.
Contactless
— a teacher sends a signature request to the guardian's own phone, and the guardian signs from the curb or the parking lot.
Admin dashboard
— you can check students in and out, or correct existing records, from the
Students
tab on web.
💡 Signatures collected on a Kiosk or on a guardian's phone carry the same weight as a teacher-recorded check-in. You can require signatures for drop-off, pick-up, or both on a per-classroom basis.
For the full walkthrough of each method, see
Log & Manage Student Attendance
,
Student Attendance for Teachers
, and
Manage Student Attendance as a Family
.
Student Attendance
Your
Students
tab is the fastest place to see where everyone stands right now. The summary tiles at the top show how many students are
Signed in
,
Signed out
, and
Absent
today, and the
Attendance status
column tags each student as
Checked in
or
Checked out
. On mobile, teachers see the same thing as colored dots on the
Roster
— green for checked-in, red for checked-out, orange for absent.
To dig into one child's history, open their profile and go to the
Activity
tab. You'll find three logs there: the
Attendance Log
for daily check-ins and check-outs, the
Program attendance log
for program check-ins, and the
Transition Log
for classroom movement throughout the day.
Anything that looks wrong can be fixed from that same log. Opening a record gives you an
Edit times
window where you can adjust a check-in, add a missing check-out, or delete a record entirely.
Absences
An absence is Playground's way of saying "this child was scheduled today but isn't coming." Admins and teachers can record one from a student's profile, and families can submit their own from the mobile app if you've turned that on.
Absences come in three flavors —
Full Day
,
Partial Day
, and
Multiple Days
. A full-day absence marks the student absent on their schedule and overrides any hours they were logged as present. A partial-day absence splits their scheduled day around the absence window and leaves their check-in status as-is.
Recording an absence also creates an absence post on the
Feed
, which is how families see it. Deleting that post removes the absence.
Staff Attendance
Staff record their own time with
clock-in
and
clock-out
, just like families do with their students.
To review or correct anyone's time, open the
Staff
tab, pick a staff member, and go to the
Hours
tab. The
History
section lists every clock-in and clock-out, and the
Record
menu lets you add hours, log a training block, or record time off. Breaks are calculated automatically from the gap between a clock-out and the next clock-in, and they're excluded from both regular and billable hours.
Full detail lives in
Manage & Edit Staff Attendance
and
Staff Schedules, Hours Reports, & Approving Time Off/PTO
.
Live Attendance Views
Your
Dashboard
has three tabs that read directly off today's attendance, so you don't have to run a report to know what's happening on the floor.
Ratios
— groups your classrooms by ratio status based on who's currently checked in and clocked in, showing
Current
versus
Max
, plus
Staff
and
Students
counts for each room. Rooms without a ratio target land under
No ratio set
.
Clocked hours
— today's staff
Hours
,
Time off
,
Billable hours
, and
Billable amount
, with arrows to step through other days.
On my way
— families who've signaled they're en route for pick-up.
Your
Classrooms
tab gives you the same picture room by room, with
Students
,
Checked in
, and
Present ratio
columns and a banner when a room is over capacity.
Related reading:
Manage Classroom Ratios
,
Name to Face
, and
On My Way Queue For Admin
.
Attendance Reports
Everything attendance-related is grouped under the
Attendance
category on your
Reports
tab. Pick a
Report Type
, set your date range, and click
Generate Report
— then
Print
for licensing or
Export
to pull the data into a spreadsheet.
A few you'll reach for most often:
Report Name
Description
Use Case
Attendance - Daily
The licensing standard — sign-in/out with the signature of whoever signed, including morning and afternoon.
View when students were checked in and out on specific days.
Attendance - Total time
How long each student was checked in per day.
Attendance Percentage
Attendance rates across a date range.
Useful for enrollment and utilization questions.
Scheduled hours
Scheduled hours compared against hours actually attended.
Ratio
Staff-to-student ratio broken out by time interval.
View which staff and students were checked into classrooms at specific times.
Daily Absences
/
No Shows
Who was marked absent, and who was scheduled but never checked in.
View which students aren't attending when they should be.
Blank Attendance
A printable paper sheet for days when you need to sign in by hand.
Staff time has its own home under the
Staff Payroll
reports. See
Attendance Reports
for the complete list and
Payroll Reports
for staff hours.
Attendance Settings
Your attendance rules live in the
Attendance
section of
Settings
→
General
. This is where you decide how much families can do on their own and how signing works at your door.
Kiosk Mode
— launch
Open kiosk mode
, download the QR code for printing, and turn on
Refresh QR code every minute
if you want the code to rotate.
School PIN code
— the code that locks and unlocks Kiosk Mode. This is separate from anyone's personal signing PIN.
Wellness check
— require guardians to complete a health screening when signing in.
Contactless (Sign-in/out)
— control whether signature requests can go to a guardian's own device instead of the school device.
Parents can mark absences
— let families record absences for their own students.
On my way queue
— turn the pick-up queue on or off.
Staff-side controls sit a little further down the same page under
Teacher permissions
— including
Track staff current classroom
,
Restrict app access while clocked out
,
Restrict teacher check in hours
,
Round staff hours
, and
Staff hours close date
. The
Geofence
setting near the top of
Settings
→
General
is what limits mobile check-ins to your physical location.
🚨
Round staff hours
and
Staff hours close date
both affect historical records. Turning on rounding updates past timesheets, and setting a close date blocks edits to staff hours on or before that date.
Where Attendance Data Goes
Attendance is one of the most connected pieces of data in Playground. Once a check-in is recorded, it shows up in several other places automatically.
Ratios & capacity
Live ratio calculations use who is currently checked in and clocked in.
Billing
Time checked in past a student's scheduled hours drives overage fees, and absences open slots for drop-in care bookings.
Subsidy
Attendance records are the source for the data you submit to subsidy agencies.
Payroll
Staff clock-ins flow into
Playground Payroll
, and paid absences carry over automatically.
Door locks
Where connected, a Kiosk sign-in can trigger your door hardware.
Because so much depends on it, clean attendance is worth the attention. If your numbers look off,
Resolve discrepancies and errors in attendance reports
is the place to start.
