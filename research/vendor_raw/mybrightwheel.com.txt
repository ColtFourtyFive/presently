How we calculated this
Estimates reflect separate, non-overlapping tasks. Monthly savings = (total admin hours × $18/hr assumed avg ECE staff wage) + paper & printing costs.
Hourly rate: assumed at $18/hr, the midpoint of the typical ECE staff range ($13-$24/hr). Adjust for your program in future iterations.
Check-in/out: ~1-1.5 min per child per direction × 2 × 22 operating days/month.
Late payment follow-up: ~10 min per late-paying family/month — assumes a mix of payment methods including reminder texts, calls, and reconciliation.
