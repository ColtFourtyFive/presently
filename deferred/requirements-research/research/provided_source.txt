Student Check-In / Check-Out System Requirements  
and Non-Exhaustive Informational Vendor List 
 
This document outlines the baseline check-in/check-out system requirements and provides a non-
exhaustive vendor reference list to help franchisees begin their own research. Franchisees may 
select any system that meets Kumon system requirements and applicable legal/compliance 
obligations; they are not limited to the vendors listed below. 
 
Once requirements are finalized and implemented, Centers will be expected to certify annually that 
their check-in/check-out system meets the applicable requirements, and centers may be asked to 
demonstrate the system during applicable review processes. 
 
Baseline Requirements Checklist 
 
Requirement Franchisee / Center Confirmation 
1. Digital System Required ☐ The center uses a digital check-in/check-out system. Manual logs, 
paper-based methods, or Excel-only tracking are not used as the 
baseline method. 
2. Unique Student 
Identification 
☐ Each student is uniquely identifiable within the system, such as by 
name, student ID, barcode, PIN, QR code, or another consistent 
identifier. 
3. Actual Arrival and Departure ☐ Check-in and check-out entries reflect actual student arrival and 
departure and are not entered later only for recordkeeping purposes. 
4. Staff Oversight and Training ☐ Staff understand the check-in/check-out process and consistently 
follow it as part of daily center operations. 
5. Current Student Awareness ☐ Staff can determine who is currently present when needed and can 
access historical attendance records for review when needed. 
6. Backup or Data Preservation 
Approach 
☐ The center has a reasonable approach to preserve necessary 
attendance data or maintain access to check-in/check-out information if 
the primary system is unavailable. 
7. Appropriate Handling of 
Student Information / 
Personally Identifiable 
Information (PII) 
☐ Student information, including personally identifiable information (PII), 
is protected and limited to what is reasonably needed to support the 
check-in/check-out workflow. 
8. Reviewable and Retained 
Records 
☐ Attendance records are reviewable when needed and retained for at 
least two years.
