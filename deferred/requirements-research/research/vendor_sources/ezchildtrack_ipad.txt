iPad Interface
iPad users can record attendance on a daily basis in a hassle-free manner avoiding any form of paper work using the EZChildTrack software’s mobile interface. Enter attendance right from your iPad without the need of being glued onto your desktop.
The EZChildTrack software's iPad interface features are summarized below:
Record attendance (check-in/check-out time) on a daily basis
Real-time attendance entry
Check-in or check-out multiple participants at one go
Easy-to-use interface
Works in collaboration with the
main EZChildTrack application
Avoid tedious paper work involving attendance data entry
