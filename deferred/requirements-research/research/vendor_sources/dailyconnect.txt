One Platform for Your Entire Business
Parent Communication
Use the Daily Connect mobile app to post updates including photos, videos, activities, meals, and more so parents know exactly what their kids are up too. Parents will be more relaxed knowing their child is in your expert care.
Learn More
Sign-In & Attendance Tracking
Parents sign their child in using a QR or PIN code. You'll always know which children are at your center and which classroom they're in. Attendance is automatically stored to keep your center compliant with licensing requirements.
Learn More
Parent Billing & Financial Reports
Automated billing tools built especially for childcare businesses. Send invoices, accept online payments, and get paid on time with payment reminder emails. Track performance with a built-in financial dashboard.
Learn More
Learning & Assessment
Record observations and assessments using your own learning framework or one of the built-in state standards. Prepare lesson plans and facilitate parent-teacher conferences with unique digital portfolios for every child.
Learn More
Staff Management & Scheduling
Create weekly schedules, track hours and time off, and maintain proper staffing ratios in every classroom. Daily Connect simplifies the administrative side of managing your team members so you can focus on the children.
Learn More
Center Administration
Track classroom activities, manage enrollment and waitlists, send checklists to teachers, capture incident reports, and produce compliance docs. Everything you need to keep your center organized and licensed.
Learn More
Smart Assistant
Draft parent messages in seconds, translate updates into any family's language, and generate ready-to-use lesson plans in minutes. Our built-in AI tools take the busywork off your staff's plate so they can focus on the children.
Learn More
Digital Enrollment Tools
Set up an online storefront, manage waitlists, and onboard new families without the paperwork. Collect customizable forms, gather digital signatures, and accept deposits all in one place — so enrollment runs smoothly.
Learn More
Licensing & Compliance
Keep attendance logs, incident reports, immunization records, and classroom ratios organized and audit-ready. Daily Connect stores everything your licensing body requires in one centralized system, so you’re always prepared.
Learn More
