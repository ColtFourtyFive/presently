Where to Find a Child’s Check-In and Check-Out Times
You can view when a child was checked in or out from their
Activity
feed on their profile. This shows a timestamp for each check-in and check-out action.
💡 If a child is marked as sick after they have already been checked in for the day, the system will display
Checked out by Famly
You can also find check-in and check-out information in the
Child attendance
.
Editing a Child’s Check-In and Check-Out Times
If you need to update a child’s check-in or check-out time, this can be done from
Child attendance
.
To edit the times:
Go to
Attendance
in your sidebar and select
Child attendance
Use the date picker at the top and click in any cell to edit a child's check-in information for that day
A pop-up window will appear. From here, you can:
Click the pen icon to edit existing check-in or check-out times
Add new check-in or check-out times
Remove check-in or check-out times
Add a vacation day
Add a sick day
Once you have made the necessary changes, click
Update
to save them.
