What is Digital Childcare?
Digital Childcare is a comprehensive, cloud-based platform specifically designed to streamline the daily operations of childcare centers. Unlike other solutions like HiMama, Brightwheel, and Procare, which offer basic functionalities, Digital Childcare delivers a full suite of features aimed at both administrators and educators, including robust billing, reporting, communication, and health tracking tools. These features help administrators save time and improve operational efficiency, making it the ideal choice for growing childcare businesses.
What are the key features of Digital Childcare?
Digital Childcare stands out with a rich set of features tailored to childcare administrators:
 Advanced Billing & Invoicing: Customize billing cycles, and automate payments with discounts.
 Parent Communication Tools: Send real-time updates, photos, and notifications through the parent portal.
 Daily Reports & Attendance Tracking: Streamline attendance with automated daily reports that track children's activities and presence.
 Health & Immunization Management: Keep track of allergies, immunization records, and health checks for each child.
 Employee Management: Manage staff schedules, track certifications, and ensure compliance with regulations.
 Compliance & Regulatory Tools: Tools to help ensure your center meets all local, provincial, and federal regulations.
Why is Digital Childcare better than other platforms?
Unlike HiMama, Brightwheel, and Procare, Digital Childcare is designed with administrators in mind, offering greater flexibility, automation, and comprehensive features:
 In-depth Reporting & Analytics: Unlike competitors, we offer customizable reports that allow you to track everything from billing data to child progress, making it easier for administrators to make data-driven decisions.
 Regulatory Compliance Assistance: Digital Childcare provides built-in compliance checks, which are more detailed than Procare and help ensure that your childcare center is always up to date with industry regulations.
How does Digital Childcare improve parent engagement?
With Digital Childcare, parent engagement goes beyond simple messaging. Administrators can create and share detailed progress reports, photos, and updates directly with parents in real time, ensuring greater transparency and communication. This level of interaction is far superior to the basic communication offered by Procare, Brightwheel, and HiMama.
How does Digital Childcare support compliance and regulatory standards?
Digital Childcare makes compliance a breeze with tools designed to help childcare providers meet all local, provincial, and federal regulations. Whether it’s tracking staff certifications or ensuring that daily child attendance is properly recorded, our platform ensures that your center stays compliant—saving you time and reducing the risk of errors.
What sets Digital Childcare apart from HiMama, Brightwheel, and Procare?
Digital Childcare is specifically built for administrators who need powerful tools for managing complex childcare operations. Detailed financial and health reports,
