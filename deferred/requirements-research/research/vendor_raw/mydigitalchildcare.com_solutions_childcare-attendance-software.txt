How does Digital Childcare simplify attendance tracking?
Digital Childcare provides a real-time attendance tracking system that is easy to use for both educators and administrators. It eliminates the need for paper logs by allowing check-ins and check-outs to be recorded digitally, ensuring accurate and secure records.
Can attendance reports be automated?
Yes! Digital Childcare offers automated monthly attendance reports that can be shared with third parties, such as government agencies, licensing bodies, or funding organizations. This feature saves time and ensures accuracy in compliance reporting.
What devices can be used for attendance tracking?
Attendance can be tracked on any device with internet access, including tablets, smartphones, and desktops. The system is user-friendly, making it easy for staff and parents to check children in and out.
Does the platform handle multiple classrooms or locations?
Absolutely! Digital Childcare is designed to support multiple classrooms and locations, making it ideal for larger centers or organizations with multiple sites. Attendance can be managed and monitored seamlessly from one central dashboard.
How does automated attendance tracking benefit childcare centers?
With Digital Childcare, centers can:

Reduce errors in attendance records.

Save time on manual tracking and reporting.

Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Provide accurate records for audits, funding applications, and parent communication.

How does Safe Arrival work?
Educators mark attendance digitally, and if a child is unexpectedly absent, the parent receives an instant push notification to confirm the absence.
What are Live Room Ratios?
Live Room Ratios update in real time based on staff clock-ins and child attendance, giving you instant visibility into ratios for every room.
