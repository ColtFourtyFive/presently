# Kumon CRM document plan

Three editable Word reports, version 0.1, dated 14 September 2026, prepared for the center owner and delivery team. Formal requirements reports with black headings, readable 11 point text, compact matrices, repeated table headers, page numbers, and direct source links. No invented corporate logo or approval status.

Working assumptions are one independently operated center at launch, future multi-center support, staff-operated MVP, and FDR means Functional Design Report. Location, center count, existing systems, volumes, budget and integration permissions are unconfirmed. User was asked asynchronously at the start. Requirements derived from the attachment are distinct from recommendations and assumptions.

BRD owns business outcomes, scope, vendor research, costs, risks, open decisions and the source register. FRD owns numbered functional and nonfunctional requirements, acceptance scenarios and traceability. FDR owns the proposed design, data model, state transitions, outage protocol, permissions, interfaces, operations and rollout. All three are drafts for review, not a claim of final Kumon or legal approval.
