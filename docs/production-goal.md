- Deliver customer-owned source, hosting, data, recovery keys, operating instructions, and update procedures.
- Preserve at least two years of reviewable attendance evidence, including original observations and later corrections.

- Do not enable source eviction before holds, expiry, restoration, and eviction evidence all pass.
